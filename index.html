Velora Health

Clinic and patient management platform by SuraTECH

Velora Health is a software platform designed to support clinic operations, patient information management, clinical documentation, nursing workflows, and authorized healthcare personnel.

Patient Management

Velora Health provides structured tools for managing patient information, visits, clinical records, vital signs, nursing care plans, tasks, notes, and attachments according to the permissions assigned to each authorized user.

Multi-Device Workflow

Velora Health is designed to support authorized clinic users across supported devices, including Android and Windows, while maintaining controlled access to clinic information.

Offline and Synchronization

The application is designed around a local-first workflow, allowing supported application functions to continue locally while synchronization services maintain authorized data consistency when connectivity is available.

Google Drive Backup and Restore

Authorized users may optionally connect a Google account through Google OAuth to use Google Drive for supported backup and restore functionality.

Access to Google services is requested only when required for the functionality selected by the user and is subject to the permissions granted by that user.

Important: Velora Health is an information management and workflow platform. It does not replace professional clinical judgment, diagnosis, treatment decisions, or the responsibilities of qualified healthcare professionals.
